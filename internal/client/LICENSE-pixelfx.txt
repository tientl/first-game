The combat effects and the fire of this package (fx.go, fireart.go) follow
the recipes of pixelfx (https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx): their timings,
shapes and palettes are taken from its effects impacts/hit-spark,
impacts/crit-hit, impacts/hit-flash, impacts/parry, melee/claw-swipe,
melee/sword-slash, melee/thrust, melee/spear-sweep, melee/heavy-cleave,
water/splash, fire/campfire and fire/torch, and redrawn in Go. pixelfx is
used under the MIT License, given in full below; parts of pixelfx are
themselves adapted from Sprite Fusion's pixel-art effects
(https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx), also under the MIT License.
